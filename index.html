How We Work

A new shop, run to a standard older than the shop. Sahni Precision Systems is a Texas company formed in 2026 and owner-operated. We build the quality record — inspection, calibration, and material traceability — from part one, so it is there when a customer asks for it.

  • →Machined to the print.You give us the drawing or model and the tolerances; we hold them or we tell you before we cut.
